The blog runs on a deliberately small static pipeline: a TypeScript manifest of posts and a typed authors registry are the single sources of truth; pure renderers turn those into the committed index and authors pages; a two-stage Docker build serves only an explicit whitelist of files.

Multi-author by construction

Every post names an author, and every author is a first-class entry in the registry — display name, bio, and link. There are no anonymous posts. That is the whole reason this scaffold diverges from the reference pattern it mirrors.

What happens next

Real copy replaces this placeholder once the editorial identity in #1 is settled. Adding a post is one manifest entry plus one HTML file under blog/, then a rebuild — no CMS, no runtime.
