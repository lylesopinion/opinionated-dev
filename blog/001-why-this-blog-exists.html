Why this blog exists

By Lyle Shemer · October 9, 2026

Draft — pending approval. This is the first real post, but it is not final. Lyle has not signed off on this copy yet. Treat it as a draft, not as a published statement.

I run a business blog. It is careful, measured, and deliberately corporate — it says "we" a lot, it never gets angry, and every sentence has been through a committee of one. That blog is useful. It is also not where I get to be a person.

This is. opinionated.dev is my personal blog, and the name is the license: it is where I say what I actually think, in the first person, without laundering it into brand-safe language first.

Why does that need to be separate? Because the two jobs pull in opposite directions. A business blog optimizes for not scaring anyone — customers, partners, the person who signs the contract. A personal blog optimizes for being worth reading. If I edited this one the way I edit the other, you would not bother, and neither would I. So they live in different places, under different rules, and I stop pretending they are the same voice.

What to expect

Strong opinions, loosely guarded. I will take positions — about software, systems, tools, causes, music, and whatever else I have thought about long enough to have a view. I will try to be right, and I will be wrong sometimes. That is the deal: opinionated, but not dogmatic; confident enough to say something, open enough to be argued with.

"Loosely guarded" is doing real work in that sentence. I am not interested in the kind of writing that hedges every claim until it means nothing. If I think something is a bad idea, I will say it is a bad idea. But I am equally not interested in pretending to certainty I do not have. When I am guessing, I will tell you I am guessing. When I change my mind, I will say so out loud.

What you will not get here: hot takes I have not thought through, and the calmer business register that belongs on the other site. This is thinking out loud, and it reads like it.

Write back

Guests write here too. This is not a solo project pretending to be one — if you have something to say and a strong opinion to say it with, send it. The path is a pull request: fork the repo, add your post through the same small pipeline every post here uses, and open the PR. Nothing is anonymous, and guests are always attributed, so you get credit rather than a soapbox.

The full flow — the author registry, the post template, the house rules — is in CONTRIBUTING.md. Read it, write something you actually mean, and send it over.

That is the whole point of this place. A personal blog, in my own voice, with the guard rails off — and an open door for anyone who wants to argue with me in public. I would rather have that conversation here than in a comment section I do not control.
